## TOP STORIES  

### **Flydubai Co-Pilot Attacked Captain with Axe**  
**What happened:** Hamam al-Hammami, a co-pilot on an Israel-bound flight, allegedly attacked the captain with an axe during the flight. The incident occurred mid-air, leading to an emergency landing.  
**Key details:** The attack took place on a Flydubai flight bound for Tel Aviv. Al-Hammami was apprehended upon landing, and the captain survived the assault.  
**Why it matters:** This unprecedented in-flight attack highlights security risks in aviation and raises questions about cockpit access protocols.  
**What happens next:** Al-Hammami is in custody, and investigations into the incident are ongoing.  
**Sources:** [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c61wv7lgex13o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### **G7 Agrees to Release Oil and Diesel to Counter Price Spikes**  
**What happened:** G7 nations have agreed to release 100 million barrels of oil and diesel to stabilize global energy markets.
